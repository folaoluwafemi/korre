## Fail-closed physical envelope

Use the existing conservative real-native-property derivation from the accepted diagnostic guard implementation. Before each call, a conservative whole-collider swept bound must prove every body stays inside the open support apron and above the slab underside, within its candidate-relative drop bound. Recheck all native state and the shifted-energy bound after the call. Unknown/unsupported geometry, native properties, contact material, solver bound or sweep returns typed `resourceLimit` and stops the matrix without a successful artifact. Cancellation, any call/batch/wall excess or output cap returns a resource-limit/cancel result and writes no success artifact. These limits bound work; they do not guarantee a case will fit.

Every actual collision callback is preserved with nominal tick, actual substep, canonical pair, bounded event type and finite point or null; do not deduplicate or truncate. Keep full-precision input, native-property, material and final-settlement evidence.

Enforce before append: no more than 8,192 contact rows per case or 196,608 total; UTF-8 event types at most 64 bytes; every body/contact/sweep row at most 512 UTF-8 bytes; every case's fixed metadata at most 64 KiB; each complete case at most 19 MiB; matrix envelope at most 64 KiB; complete compact UTF-8 JSON at most 457 MiB. The arithmetic is `(27,015 state + 1,801 sweep + 8,192 contact) × 512 bytes = 18,948,096 bytes` per case, plus at most 64 KiB metadata, below 19 MiB; 24 cases plus a 64 KiB matrix envelope are below 457 MiB. Check actual serialized bytes, not estimated object size. Stream to a temporary output, hash the complete encoded file, and atomically rename only after all 24 cases, checksums, tuple-order checks and assertions pass. Any row, contact, metadata, case, aggregate-byte, call, batch or time cap fails the entire matrix; never shorten data or leave a partial file marked successful.

Each case may be marked eligible only when its whole button extent starts before and finishes beyond the obstacle, conservative full-button swept clearance exceeds the separately named vertical diagnostic margin `0.005` throughout the full horizontal-overlap interval, no actual button/obstacle contact occurs, all 15 bodies remain finite and settle for the canonical 0.5-second window, and every actual substep passes the hashed guard. Zero eligible cases is valid only when all 24 trials and their complete artifact assertions pass. A collision, non-crossing, or failure to settle may make a completed case ineligible; guard, provenance, workload, malformed-output or integrity failure aborts the whole matrix.
