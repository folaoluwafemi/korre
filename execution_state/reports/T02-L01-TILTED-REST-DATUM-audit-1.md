## Bounded safe datum and unchanged limits

A conservative orientation-independent lower datum follows from the actual cylinder support geometry. Let `b` be the midpoint of the raw native local AABB, `c` the raw native local COM offset, `r` the native cylinder radius, `q` its native half-height, and `p` the canonical pitch plane. For cylinder axis unit vector `n`, the vertical support distance below the AABB center is `r*sqrt(1-n.y^2) + q*abs(n.y)`, which is at least `min(r,q)`. The COM is at most `norm(c-b)` below that center in any orientation. Thus every nonpenetrating orientation satisfies:

```text
restDatumY = p + min(r, q) - norm(c - b)
```

For the canonical centered shapes, a strict `b === (0,0,0)` preflight lets the runner use the already-recorded raw dimensions and COM offset; reject unsupported shape type, nonfinite values, or a noncentered native AABB before impulse. If the coordinator prefers to allow nonzero `b`, serialize the raw midpoint/bounds and make the validator recompute the datum from them. Do not silently treat an unrecorded midpoint as zero.

Use `p = surfaceY` only after a finite exact preflight proves the **actual native apron top** (`supportBody.position.y + nativeLocalAabb.maximum.y`) is at or above `surfaceY`. The existing support check validates the center transform and projected Y dimension, but does not explicitly check this world-top relation. With the configured center `-0.1` and projected thickness `Math.fround(0.2)=0.20000000298023224`, the centered-box calculation gives top `1.4901161e-9`, slightly above `surfaceY=0`; retain the exact native readback and fail closed if that relation does not hold. This makes the configured plane a conservative lower support plane without inventing an epsilon.

Keep the individual drop limit `0.01125`, energy ceiling `34`, impulse, gravity, full-collider sweep limits, 48-call cap, apron, and workload unchanged. Define energy at every observed and predicted state as:

```text
Eshift = sum_i(K_i + m_i*g*(COMy_i - (restDatumY_i - 0.01125)))
```

Require every body's COM at or above `restDatumY_i - 0.01125`; retain the same pre-call swept bound and post-call rechecks, now measured from this datum. A pre-impulse COM need not equal the floor datum: it must match the hashed initial transform's actual native COM, have exactly zero velocity, and pass the same energy check. The tilted button's initial elevation is real potential energy and must be included, not subtracted as a free pose allowance.

The configured-value arithmetic shows that no ceiling increase is needed in the two frozen tilt cases. At `J=8`, `m_home-0=1`, total configured dynamic mass `14.15`, and button mass `0.15`, estimated post-impulse shifted energy is `32 + 14.15*9.81*0.01125 + 0.15*9.81*(initialButtonY-0.0225)`: `33.6871980` at 45° and `33.7107413` at 60°, below 34. This is a calculation from configured values, **not** a native-runtime result. The implementation must calculate with actual finite native masses, inertias, COMs and dimensions and fail before impulse if the exact measured energy exceeds 34. The sweep formula and step subdivision remain unchanged because they continue to use the same 34 energy ceiling, gravity, drop allowance and actual shape facts.
